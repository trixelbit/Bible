Summary/Notes:


    C1: 

        When: 8th month, 2nd Year, Darius 
        Who: Zechariah <- Berechiah <- Iddo 

        Yahweh was displeased with his fathers.

        3 Times, God is refered to as "Yahweh of armies". 
        I guess to signify his military powers. 
        The fact that he is the one who delivers victories, not man.

        He tells Zechariah to tell his fathers to return to him,
        and that in turn, he will return to them.

        He also tells Zechariah to not be like his fathers, 
        who heard God's warning but did not listen. 

        He say prophets don't live forever= but God's decrees 
        do come true. 

        In these case of his fathers, they came to regret no listening 
        to His Warnings. 

        When: 24th day, 11th month, 2nd years, Darius  (the Shebat)
        Zechariah received a vision:
            
            A man riding on a red horse who stoff among myrtle trees 
            in a ravine. Behind him there were red, brown, and white horses.

            Zechariah asked what are these.

            The angel said he will show him. 

            The man in the myrtle trees answered,
            "They are ones Yahweh has sent to go back and forth 
            through the earth."

            They reported to the angel of Yahweh who stood in th myrtle tree
            and said they walked back and forth through the earth and it was 
            at peace.
