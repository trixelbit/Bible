



# Chapter 2 


## 18

    Calls us to not lose prize by worhsip things like angels,
    but instead to be focus on the head (Christ).

    Paul also highlights that the focus should be on Christ,
    so we should not be obsessive about the laws like the people 
    of the world are.

    In this case, he was talking about practices and dietary laws.

    So mosaic laws about are not to be obsessed over to please God.

    Human laws are temporary things that are passing and are nothing 
    in comparison to the eternal greatness of Christ.
