




# Chapter 4

    Calls us to no believe every spirit but rather test them.

    Qualifier:
        Spirits from God confess that Jesus Christ 
        has come from God in the flesh

    People from the world:
        - Speak from the world
        - The world listens to them

    We
    - are from God
    - who knows God listens to use 
    - whoever doesn't does not listen to us.

    This is how we distinguish between Spirit of truth,
    and the spirit of error.
